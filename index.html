Fundraising Vision

Help us conserve our rare and fragile materials within Linn‑Henley Library by donating to revitalize our spaces and modernize our equipment.

$500,000 Fundraising Goal
$325,000

Community Memory Lab

The Community Memory Lab will give Birmingham families free access to professional scanners, audio transfer equipment, software, and hosting. You can bring in photographs, documents, and recordings, make digital copies, and take your originals home with you. We project that 600 families a year will use the service. The renovation also expands the Archives onto the third floor, with a quick-reference space for the most heavily used collections.

Learn More
$100,000

Rare Book Room

The Rare Book Room’s fire suppression system is obsolete and nonfunctional. A new system uses clean, next-generation extinguishing agents that are colorless, odorless, and leave no residue on library materials. The library that stood in City Hall burned in 1925. This makes sure that never happens again.

$75,000

Reading Room Renovation

The Ezra Winter murals in the reading room are being restored, and museum-quality display cases will put the Southern History Department’s rarest materials on view. When the work is complete, the reading room will open again as a place to study, discover, and work with the city’s history.

“I feel like I’m stepping back in time when I visit the Archives at the Birmingham Public Library — back into a magical, beautifully preserved space, with expert guides to help me explore hidden wonders. It’s a gem of a place, a gift to Birmingham and the world.” — Jonathan Eig, Pulitzer Prize winner for King: A Life
